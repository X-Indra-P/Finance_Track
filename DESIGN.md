## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, polished visual foundation engineered for financial clarity, wealth tracking, and portfolio stewardship. It merges institutional fiscal discipline with the refreshing modernity of high-growth fintech platforms.

### Target Audience
The system serves retail investors, modern financial planners, and personal finance managers who require clear visual hierarchies, effortless scanning of dense numerical data, and a calm, reassuring interface during complex financial workflows.

### Emotional Response
- **Confidence & Stability:** Anchored by deep, enduring emerald tones that evoke stability, steady growth, and fiscal security.
- **Clarity & Control:** Free of visual clutter, enabling immediate comprehension of net worth, cash flow trends, and transaction statuses.
- **Precision:** Meticulous spacing, refined typography, and purposeful contrast communicate reliability and technical sophistication.

### Visual Style
The design style is **Corporate Modern with Tactile Refinement**. It pairs architectural structural lines with tonal surfaces, subtle diffused shadows, and deliberate micro-borders. High-density data tables and ledger views alternate with spacious metric cards to maintain high legibility across long viewing sessions.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional authority with lively operational feedback. Deep greens convey capital stability, while crisp mints highlight upward momentum and growth metrics.

### Primary & Brand Accents
- **Primary (`#047857`):** The primary emerald hue. Used for primary interactive controls, core brand indicators, active navigation states, and confirmed states.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Fresh mint green. Applied to growth badges, positive trend indicators, secondary action accents, and progress graphs.
- **Tertiary (`#064E3B`):** Deep forest emerald. Serves as high-contrast display text, authoritative table headers, and anchor elements requiring visual weight without defaulting to pure black.

### Neutral & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** The foundational background, designed to minimize eye strain while keeping cards distinctly visible.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Base card and container surface, providing pure contrast against background canvas layers.
- **Muted Surfaces (`#F1F5F9`):** Subdued containers, table alternate-row stripes, metric pill backgrounds, and disabled controls.
- **Border Lines (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline structure for card dividers, table borders, and input boundaries.
- **Text & Neutral Anchors (`#0F172A`, `#334155`, `#64748B`):** Deep slate tones for high-legibility typographic hierarchy, ensuring zero ambiguity in numeric ledgers.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Positive / Income (`#10B981` / `#047857`):** Signals incoming capital, completed settlements, and positive variance.
- **Negative / Expense (`#EF4444`):** Soft rose/coral tone calibrated to remain distinct and urgent without overwhelming standard analytical views.
- **Warning / Pending (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber used strictly for pending settlements, alerts, and authorization gates.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent figures, balances, and structural headers with **Inter** for data tables, form controls, and multi-line descriptive text.

### Architectural Hierarchy
- **Header Structure:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides warmth and approachability without sacrificing authority. Its tighter geometric proportions create clean, confident section titles and financial hero values.
- **Analytical Precision:** Inter handles dense transactional rows, ledger items, metadata tags, and body text. Its neutral glyph construction and high x-height guarantee that decimals, currencies, and timestamps remain crisp at small sizes.

### Numeric Presentation Rules
- Always activate tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) on currency amounts, ledger values, and metric cards to ensure vertical alignment of numbers across columns.
- Currency symbols are kept at standard weight, while values utilize medium to bold weights to prioritize visual scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point geometric scale, structured around an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate wide financial dashboards and compact mobile views alike.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Dashboard metric cards collapse into single-column vertical stacks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `margin` (1.5rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Metric cards reflow into 2x2 grids; transactional tables allow horizontal scrolling with pinned key identifiers.
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout, maximum container width of 1440px, `margin-lg` (3rem), `gutter-lg` (2rem). Supports multi-pane layouts: sidebar navigation (3 columns), analytical views (6 columns), and contextual side-panels (3 columns).

### Rhythm & Density Guidelines
- **Card-level Padding:** Use `space-lg` (1.5rem) for primary analytical containers; step down to `space-md` (1rem) for condensed tables and data widgets.
- **Data Densities:** Metric groupings employ `space-xs` (0.25rem) between captions and figures. Section headings use `space-md` to clearly demarcate upcoming data tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through a hybrid of **tonal layering**, **crisp hairline borders**, and **subtle ambient shadows tinted with deep slate**. The aesthetic remains grounded and functional, preventing floaty or distracting visual artifacts during data review.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Colored `#F8FAFC`, serving as the neutral plane against which white containers rest.
- **Card Surfaces:** Solid `#FFFFFF`, outlined with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` to maintain clean structural definition regardless of display calibration.
- **Interactive Hover & Active Surfaces:** Elevated layers employ an ambient shadow with a subtle emerald-tinted blur (`rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04)` to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).

### Elevation Tokens
- **Flat (Level 0):** Used for background canvas and flat table rows. Border: none. Shadow: none.
- **Surface (Level 1):** Default state for analytical cards, metrics, and summary tiles. Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Raised (Level 2):** Hover states of interactive cards, dropdown menus, and popovers. Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Overlay (Level 3):** Modal dialogs, transfer confirmation sheets, and flyout drawer panels. Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a refined curvature scale anchored by `roundedness: 2` (base radius of 0.5rem / 8px). For high-level containment components such as financial summary cards and modular panels, the scale expands to `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to create an approachable, modern feel.

### Geometric Scale Application
- **Base Elements (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Form inputs, buttons, filter dropdowns, segmented tabs, and notification alerts.
- **Intermediate Containers (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Nested summary blocks, transfer preview panels, and table wrappers.
- **Primary Cards & Modals (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Dashboard metric cards, balance displays, hero containers, and main dialog windows.
- **Pills / Full Curve (`rounded-full`):** Category tags, status indicators, avatar circles, and progress bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#047857` with white text. Hover state shifts to `#064E3B`. Focus ring uses a 2px `#10B981` outline with a 2px white offset. Active states apply a subtle scale compression (0.99x).
- **Secondary:** White background with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `#334155` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F8FAFC` and border deepens to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background with `#047857` text. On hover, fills with a 10% tint of mint (`#ECFDF5`).
- **Destructive:** Soft rose background tint (`#FEF2F2`) with `#DC2626` text, transitioning on hover to solid `#DC2626` with white text.

### Metric & Summary Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and `rounded-xl` corner radii.
- Internal padding is set to `space-lg` (1.5rem).
- Structure includes:
  - Top row: Semantic icon in a 40x40px `#ECFDF5` container, paired with a subtle neutral label in `label-md`.
  - Middle: Primary currency balance rendered in `num-display` using tabular numbers.
  - Bottom: Trend indicator pill showcasing dynamic green/red badges with a small directional vector icon.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Income Badge:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`, `rounded-full` radius, `label-sm` typography with uppercase tracking.
- **Expense Badge:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#B91C1C`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`, `rounded-full` radius, `label-sm` typography.
- **Filter Chips:** Segmented pill controls with `#F1F5F9` neutral state and white text/emerald fill when selected.

### Transaction Lists & Data Tables
- Table headers utilize `#F8FAFC` background with `label-sm` slate text (`#64748B`), styled with subtle 1px border dividers.
- Transaction rows feature a fixed height of 56px, subtle `#F8FAFC` hover fill, and border-bottom hairline dividers (`#F1F5F9`).
- Transaction names use `title-sm` with a secondary category subtitle in `body-sm`. Positive inflows prepend a `+` in bold emerald; outflows prepend a `-` in slate/rose.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- Inputs feature a crisp 1px `#CBD5E1` border over a `#FFFFFF` fill with `rounded` (8px) corners.
- Padding: 10px horizontal, 12px vertical. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#047857` paired with an emerald ring glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.15)`).
- Prefix currency fields feature an integrated `#F1F5F9` static marker (`$`, `€`, `£`) separated by a vertical border line.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base control: 18x18px with a 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`.
- Checked state: Solid `#047857` fill containing a centered white SVG checkmark.
- Radio buttons feature standard circular geometry with a centered `#047857` dot on white fill.

### Financial Chart Containers
- Chart panels sit inside standard `rounded-xl` cards with integrated timeline filters (e.g., 1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y, ALL) in the card header.
- Area graphs utilize smooth Bezier curves with an emerald line (`#047857`) and a fading vertical gradient from `#10B981` (20% opacity) down to pure transparent at the baseline.
- Grid crosshairs use `#E2E8F0` dashed lines, with tooltip cards floating in Level 2 elevation displaying date and precise tabular currency values.
